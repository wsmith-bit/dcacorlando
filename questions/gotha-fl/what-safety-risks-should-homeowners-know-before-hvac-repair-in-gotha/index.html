Common Hazards to Occupants and Property

  • Electrical Risks: HVAC systems are wired to high-voltage electrical circuits. Any improper handling can lead to electric shocks or fires. Homeowners should never attempt to repair electrical components without proper training.
  • Refrigerant Leaks: HVAC systems use refrigerants that can be harmful if inhaled. If you notice a chemical smell or find liquid pooling around your unit, this is a sign of a leak that requires immediate professional attention.
  • Gas Lines: For homes with gas-powered heating systems, any issue with gas lines can pose serious safety risks, including explosions or poisoning. Never attempt to repair gas lines yourself; always call a licensed technician.
  • Structural Damage: Poorly maintained HVAC systems can lead to water leaks that may cause mold growth or structural deterioration over time. This is particularly crucial in humid regions like Gotha, where moisture can exacerbate these issues.
