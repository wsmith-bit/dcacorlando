Many homes in Loughman, particularly in established neighborhoods like Victoria Park, are older and may have outdated HVAC systems. These older units often lack the energy efficiency of modern systems, resulting in higher utility bills and more frequent repairs. Common issues include:

Environmental Factors

The soil and environmental conditions in Loughman can also affect HVAC systems. For instance, the region’s sandy soil can lead to issues with drainage and water accumulation around outdoor units. This can cause:
