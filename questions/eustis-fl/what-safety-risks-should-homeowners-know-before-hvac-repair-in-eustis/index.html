Common Hazards to Occupants and Property

  • Electrical Hazards: HVAC systems operate on high voltages, and improper handling can lead to electrical shocks or fires. DIY repairs often overlook essential safety protocols, increasing the risk of serious accidents.
  • Refrigerant Leaks: Many air conditioning units use refrigerants that can be harmful to both people and pets if leaked. Detecting and repairing these leaks requires specialized training and equipment.
  • Structural Risks: Inadequate sealing or installation can lead to issues such as carbon monoxide leaks from heating systems, which can be fatal. Regular inspections by a licensed technician can help prevent these dangers.

In Eustis, HVAC repairs must comply with local building codes, which are designed to ensure the safety of the system and its operation.
