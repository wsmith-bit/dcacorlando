What Good Work Looks Like

When assessing HVAC repair work, homeowners should expect the following:

  • System Performance: The HVAC system should return to normal operation without unusual noises, vibrations, or odors. In Howey In The Hills, where temperatures can soar, your AC should cool effectively and maintain comfortable humidity levels.
  • Visible Quality of Work: Inspect the installation site for neatness. Ductwork should be properly sealed and insulated, and components should be securely fastened.
  • Documentation: A detailed service report should be provided, outlining the work completed, parts replaced, and any warranties on the repairs.

Checks Before Signing Off

Before finalizing the repair work, homeowners should conduct the following checks:

  • Functionality Test: Turn on the HVAC system to ensure it operates smoothly. Check both cooling and heating functions to confirm they are working as intended.
  • Temperature Measurement: Use a thermometer to check the temperature differential between the supply and return vents. A difference of 15-20 degrees Fahrenheit indicates proper operation.
  • Inspect Filters: Ensure the air filters have been replaced or cleaned as part of the service. Clogged filters can hinder system efficiency.

Documentation to Request

It’s important to ask for documentation that supports the quality of the repair:

  • Service Warranty: Request a warranty on parts and labor. A reputable HVAC company will stand behind their work.
  • Maintenance Recommendations: Ask for a list of maintenance tasks you should perform to prolong the life of your HVAC system, especially given Howey In The Hills’ humid conditions.
  • Energy Efficiency Ratings: If any new components were installed, request documentation on their energy efficiency ratings to ensure they meet local codes and standards.

Red Flags Indicating Poor Workmanship

Be vigilant for signs of poor workmanship that could indicate problems down the road:

  • Unexplained Charges: If your invoice includes unexpected charges, this could signal a lack of transparency in pricing.
  • Delayed Repairs: If the technician was unable to complete repairs in the proposed time frame without a valid reason, this could indicate inefficiency or inexperience.
  • Inconsistent System Performance: If your HVAC system continues to malfunction or fails to meet expected performance levels shortly after repairs, this is a serious concern.
