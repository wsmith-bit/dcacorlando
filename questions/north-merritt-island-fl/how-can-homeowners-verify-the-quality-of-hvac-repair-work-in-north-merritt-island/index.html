What Good Work Looks Like

When assessing HVAC repair work, good workmanship should be evident in several areas. Look for the following indicators:

  • Clean Installation: Ducts and wires should be neatly organized, and there should be no visible signs of debris or leftover parts.
  • Functional Testing: The technician should perform tests to ensure that the system is running efficiently, including checking airflow and temperature differentials.
  • Visible Components: Key components such as filters, coils, and vents should be easily accessible and in good condition.
  • Documentation Provided: Homeowners should receive a detailed invoice that includes parts replaced, labor performed, and any warranties or guarantees.

What to Inspect Before Signing Off

Before you sign off on the repair work, take the time to inspect the following:

  • Temperature Output: Ensure that the air conditioning system is cooling effectively and that the heater is providing adequate heat, especially important during the cooler months on Merritt Island.
  • Noise Levels: Listen for unusual sounds, such as rattling or grinding, which could indicate improper installation or unresolved issues.
  • Thermostat Functionality: Check that the thermostat responds accurately to adjustments and communicates effectively with the HVAC system.

Documentation to Request

Proper documentation is critical for verifying quality and ensuring accountability. Ask for the following:

  • Service Report: A summary of the work completed, including parts replaced, repairs made, and any recommendations for future maintenance.
  • Warranty Information: Documentation regarding any warranties on parts and labor, which can provide peace of mind in case of future issues.
  • Licensing and Certification: Verify that the technicians are licensed and certified to perform HVAC work in Florida, as this ensures compliance with local codes and regulations.

Red Flags Indicating Poor Workmanship

Be vigilant for signs of subpar workmanship that could lead to future problems:

  • Inconsistent Performance: If the system fails to maintain temperature or requires frequent adjustments, this may indicate improper installation.
  • High Energy Bills: A sudden spike in energy costs following repairs could suggest inefficiencies caused by poor workmanship.
  • Lack of Communication: If the technician fails to explain the repairs or is dismissive of your questions, it may be a sign of unprofessional service.
