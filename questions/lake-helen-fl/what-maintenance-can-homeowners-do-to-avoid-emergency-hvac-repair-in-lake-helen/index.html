Seasonal Maintenance Tasks

Homeowners should establish a seasonal maintenance schedule to keep their HVAC systems in top shape. Here are some key tasks to include:

  • Change Filters Regularly: Replace or clean your air filters every 1-3 months. Clogged filters can restrict airflow, causing your system to work harder and potentially fail. This is especially crucial in Lake Helen, where pollen and humidity can exacerbate air quality issues.
  • Clear Debris: Ensure outdoor units are free of leaves, dirt, and other debris. This is particularly important after storms or heavy winds typical in the area.
  • Check Thermostat Settings: Verify that your thermostat is functioning correctly and is set to the appropriate temperatures for both heating and cooling seasons.

Monitoring Warning Signs

Being aware of warning signs can save homeowners from unexpected HVAC breakdowns. Pay attention to:

  • Unusual Noises: Banging, hissing, or grinding sounds can indicate mechanical problems.
  • Increased Energy Bills: A sudden spike in your energy bills can suggest that your system is working harder due to inefficiencies.
  • Inconsistent Temperatures: If some rooms in your Lake Helen home are significantly warmer or cooler than others, it may indicate ductwork issues or an aging system.
